Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fergotskills Training (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Fergotskills Training mobile application on Android and iOS.

Fergotskills Training is a calisthenics training app from coach Fergo Skills. Athletes use it to follow assigned programmes — including one-on-one coaching, skill-focused plans, and general level programming — and to track workout progress. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to users of the Fergotskills Training mobile app. The developer / operator named in the app store listing is responsible for the processing described here.

Privacy contact: f.prieto.moyano@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of data:

  • Account information — email address, user ID, and authentication credentials managed through Firebase Authentication. Accounts are typically created or provisioned by a coach; you sign in with the email and password provided for your account.
  • Profile information — display name associated with your athlete profile, when available.
  • Training and programme data — assigned programmes, workout structure, task completion status, and related progress you record in the App. This data is stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Device and usage data (production builds) — in production releases, we may collect analytics events (for example screen views and product usage) and crash / diagnostic reports via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may also process device identifiers, app version, OS version, and similar technical data needed to operate analytics and crash reporting.
  • Network / security data — the App uses an internet connection and Firebase App Check to help protect backend resources from abuse. This may involve device attestation signals processed by Google / Firebase.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, or similar sensitive device permissions for its core training features.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure
  • To show your assigned training programmes and sync progress
  • To allow password reset emails when requested
  • To monitor reliability, diagnose crashes, and improve the App (production builds)
  • To protect the service against abuse and unauthorized access
  • To respond to support requests you send us

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as needed to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, and App Check. Google processes data as a service provider according to its terms and privacy documentation.
  • Your coach / training organisation — coaches or admins who manage your programme assignment may access account, profile, and training progress data needed to deliver coaching.
  • Legal / safety — if required to comply with law, enforce terms, or protect users, the App, or others.

5. Data storage and retention

Account, profile, and training data are stored in Firebase services. We retain this information for as long as your account remains active and it is needed to provide the App, or until deletion is requested and completed (subject to any short-term backups or legal retention obligations).

Analytics and crash data may be retained by Firebase according to Google’s product retention settings and our configuration.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to a cloud-backed mobile app, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), Firebase Authentication, security rules on Firestore data, and App Check in supported environments. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Account and data deletion

If you want your Fergotskills Training account and associated personal data deleted, contact us at f.prieto.moyano@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, or ask your coach to initiate deletion on your behalf.

After verifying the request, we will delete your account authentication record and associated personal / progress data that we control, except where we must retain limited information for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.

If Google Play requires a public web deletion URL, use this page’s contact process above until a dedicated deletion form is published.

8. Children’s privacy

Fergotskills Training is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International processing

Firebase / Google may process data in the United States and other countries where Google or its subprocessors operate. If you use the App from the UK / EEA or another region, your information may be transferred internationally subject to applicable safeguards offered by those providers.
